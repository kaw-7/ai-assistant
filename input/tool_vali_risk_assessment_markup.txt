General idea:
Any build/compiler/linker or other errors given as issues in the release notes are easily detected by developers or pipeline build 
and would be recognized immediately so those are of no risk. 
Also any other issues that are easily detected by developers and other personel are of no risk. 
E.g. debugging issues are present only during development, thus, such issues affecting debugging workflow,
cannot manifest on a deployed version of any binary output and are of no risk.
An issue imposing a risk would be one that could create a corrupt binary without the knowledge of the developer. 
Or a binary which works with hidden bugs because of e.g. compiler issues (e.g. the compiler does improper division by 0.0 
resulting again in 0.0 is considered a bug with high risk) is a risk.
